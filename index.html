Your private paradise begins at the door.

Description

Step into your own private escape at Casa Lucky — where a secluded outdoor retreat with plunge pool, warm evenings under the stars, and a 2-minute walk to one of Huatulco's calmest beaches set the tone. Designed for effortless indoor-outdoor living, this is where slow mornings, sun-filled afternoons, and relaxed evenings come naturally. From beach days to quiet nights by the water, Casa Lucky offers a setting that feels both private and connected — exactly what a Huatulco escape should be.

Your Property

The Experience

Step directly into your own private outdoor retreat — no hallways, no shared entry, just your patio, plunge pool, and the start of your stay.

Start your mornings with coffee as the birds come to life across the Sierra Madre hills that frame Huatulco. Walk just minutes to Santa Cruz Beach — known for its calm, swimmable water — where you can spread out with the beach setup we provide or enjoy drinks at one of the beachfront restaurants.

Spend the afternoon by the pool or exploring the marina and nearby shops. Then return home, fire up your private grill, and unwind in your plunge pool as the evening lights of Santa Cruz begin to glow.

Your Private Retreat

Casa Lucky welcomes you a little differently — intentionally designed for a more private, seamless stay. Instead of a traditional interior entry, your front door opens directly into your own private outdoor retreat — a secluded patio designed for relaxation from the moment you arrive.

Here, you'll find:

  • Private plunge pool with two premium lounge chairs and umbrella
  • Outdoor dining area with a separate shaded umbrella
  • Electric grill for easy, relaxed evenings

The space is thoughtfully enclosed to feel both open and private. Lush hedge greenery lines the front wall, while decorative side panels and plantings create a natural sense of seclusion. The architectural lattice (celosía-style) brickwork allows light and air to flow through while maintaining a tucked-away, peaceful atmosphere.

From the patio, step inside to a bright, open-concept living space:

  • Comfortable living area with Smart TV
  • Fully equipped kitchen with quartz island and bar seating
  • High-speed Starlink Wi-Fi

The king bedroom offers a quiet retreat with its own Smart TV — and, like the living room, opens directly onto the patio, creating a seamless indoor-outdoor flow. Whether you're starting your morning outside, slipping into the pool in the afternoon, or winding down under soft evening light, the entire space is designed to feel effortless, private, and distinctly yours.

Thoughtful Touches

We've made it effortless to enjoy the beach lifestyle:

  • Beach towels ready to go
  • Pickleball rackets and balls for two

No packing, no planning — just grab and walk.

Description

Step into Casa Racél — where bold Mexican color, original artwork, and elevated Sierra Madre views set a tone all its own. One floor up from the ground level, your private balcony opens to alfresco dining for six and panoramic views — jungle treetops, distant harbor lights, and sunsets over the mountains. Designed for families and groups who want both space and personality, Casa Racél offers two full bedrooms, two spa-style baths, and an open-concept living area built for long mornings, easy afternoons, and slow evenings together.

Your Property

The Experience

Wake to morning light filtering through the lattice screens as you brew coffee in the open kitchen. Step onto the private balcony — your Sierra Madre panorama rolls out from the treetops to the harbor below — and let the day begin slowly, with breakfast for six just steps away.

Walk to Santa Cruz Beach in just 2–3 minutes — known for its calm, swimmable water — where you can spread out with the beach setup we provide or settle in for drinks at a beachfront restaurant. Afternoons drift between the community pool, the marina, and the shaded balcony upstairs.

As the sun lowers, Casa Racél's balcony comes into its own. Watch the light shift over the Sierra Madre, dine alfresco beneath the jungle canopy, and let evening settle in with the warm glow of Santa Cruz on the hillside.

A Home That's Boldly Itself

Casa Racél is named for two women — Mateo's Nana Rachel and Francisco's Mamá Celina — and the home reflects them: strong, vibrant, and unmistakably themselves. Original Mexican artwork lines every wall. A signature Frida portrait anchors the living area. A bold splash-art canvas frames the balcony entrance. Mosaic-frame mirrors, embroidered pillows, mustard velvet, and lattice screen doors give Casa Racél a personality you'll remember long after you leave.

Here, you'll find:

  • Living area sleeper sofa accommodates additional guests — sleeps 6 total

Our Story

Francisco &
Mateo Bravo

What begins with a dream of a slower, sun-drenched life in Mexico is, for Francisco and Mateo Bravo, now a reality in the making. After more than a decade managing a portfolio of rental properties in Kansas, USA — supporting dozens of tenants — they're channeling that same dedication into something far more beautiful.

Mateo holds a PhD from the University of Kansas and spent over 20 years in senior healthcare leadership. Francisco, a Mexico native, brings decades of hospitality experience and has served as their full-time property manager for years. Together they bring the professionalism of seasoned landlords and the genuine warmth of true hosts.

Francisco and Mateo are currently based in the US and plan to relocate to Mexico full time in the coming years. In the meantime, you'll find them with family at their estate in Numaran, Michoacán when not managing the business remotely.

We’re here to make your stay smooth from start to finish. All pre-arrival communication will be personally handled by us, Mateo and Francisco, so you’ll have everything you need before you arrive. Once you’re in Huatulco, our trusted local team — Javier and Osmar — will greet you upon arrival and be available throughout your stay to assist with anything you may need. This approach gives you the best of both worlds—direct connection with us as owners, along with attentive, on-the-ground support to ensure a seamless and relaxing experience.

Behind the Names

The Stories Behind
Our Casitas

Every name carries a story worth telling — of family, of love, and of lives well lived. When you stay with us, you stay in their honor.

Lucky · 13 glorious years
Casa Lucky
A Pomeranian. A legend.

Thirteen years isn't just a long time — it's a whole life built together. Lucky was small in stature and enormous in personality, the kind of dog who made every room a little brighter and every moment a little warmer. We were lucky to have him, and we knew it every single day.

Casa Lucky fits him perfectly — cozy, full of character, and absolutely the life of the party. When you're here, you're staying in the spirit of a very good boy who never wasted a single beautiful day.

Mateo & Nana Rachel
Francisco & Mamá Celina
Casa Racél
"Rac" from Nana Rachel · "Cel" from Mamá Celina

She stitched Christmas stockings by hand — he leaned into her shoulder without a word needing to be said. Racél is a name born of two families: Rac from Mateo's Nana Rachel, and Cel from Francisco's mother Celina. Two women. Two families. One name that carries them both.

The bold colors, the Frida portrait, the warmth you feel the moment you walk in — that's intentional. It's their presence, woven into every corner. Strong, vibrant, unapologetically themselves. Just like the women who inspired them.

Rico · 17 years of quiet command
Casa Rico
The leader. The legend. The highest perch.

Seventeen years. Through moves, milestones, and everything in between, Rico was always there — calm, steady, and quietly in charge. Our miniature dachshund didn't need to announce himself. He simply led, and everyone followed.

It only felt right that Casa Rico sits at the top of our Las Casitas collection — the highest floor, the widest views, the most commanding presence. Rico earned it. He always did.

Lucky and Rico had the run of the place in their day — and Diego, our miniature dachshund, will carry on that tradition when he joins us in Mexico. Our casitas aren't pet-free by nature, just by guest policy. We ask that you leave your four-legged family members at home, and we thank you for understanding.

The Destination

Why Huatulco?

Tucked along the Oaxacan coast where the Sierra Madre del Sur meets the Pacific, Huatulco is Mexico's best-kept secret.

🌊
Nine Bays, 36 Beaches
From calm swimming coves to secluded anchorages — most remain blissfully uncrowded.
⛰️
Sierra Madre Meets the Sea
The dramatic backdrop of jungle-draped mountains tumbling to the Pacific creates scenery unlike any other resort town in Mexico.
🐠
World-Class Eco-Tourism
Snorkel, dive, kayak, raft jungle rivers, hike to waterfalls. Huatulco is a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve.
🍽️
Authentic Oaxacan Culture
From the vibrant markets of La Crucecita to fresh seafood right on the sand — this is Mexico as it's meant to be.
✈️
Easily Accessible
Huatulco International Airport (HUX) is just 20 minutes away, with direct flights from Mexico City, Guadalajara, and growing international routes.

La Crucecita & Santa Cruz

Wander lively market stalls for handcrafted jewelry and Oaxacan textiles. Then follow the waterfront to the marina, where fishing boats head out at dawn and return with the day's catch.
